About Sedikana Attorneys


Professional Legal Representation, Close to the Communities We Serve
Established in 2016, Sedikana Attorneys Inc is a law firm committed to providing quality, professional and accessible legal services to individuals and communities across Tzaneen and surrounding areas of Limpopo.
Led by Lungile Usuel Sedikana, an admitted attorney, the firm was founded with a commitment to making dependable legal representation more accessible to people, particularly those in rural and underserved communities where access to professional legal services can be limited.
Over the years, Sedikana Attorneys has built its practice around understanding the circumstances of each client and providing legal guidance that is clear, practical and focused on protecting our clients’ rights and interests.
Our practice encompasses criminal matters, civil matters, unlawful arrest and detention claims, Road Accident Fund (RAF) claims, litigation and legal consultations. Whether a client is facing criminal proceedings, dealing with a civil dispute, seeking compensation following a road accident, or needs advice on their legal rights and options, we approach every matter with professionalism, confidentiality and dedication.

Serving Tzaneen and Beyond

Our commitment extends beyond the traditional boundaries of a city-centre law practice. We serve clients in Tzaneen and surrounding communities, including Nkowankowa, Lenyenye, Hoedspruit, Maake and surrounding rural areas.
With the opening of our Maake office in Burgersdorp Village, we are continuing our commitment to bringing professional legal services closer to the communities that need them.
We believe that where you live should not determine the quality of legal assistance available to you.

Our Approach

At Sedikana Attorneys, we believe that effective legal representation begins with listening. We take the time to understand our clients, their circumstances and the legal challenges they face before advising them on the way forward.
Our approach is built on three principles:

Professionalism

We approach every matter with preparation, diligence and respect for the law.

Confidentiality

We understand the sensitive nature of legal matters and treat our clients’ information and affairs with the highest level of confidentiality.

Client-Focused Service

Every client and every matter is different. We provide legal guidance tailored to the circumstances of each case and remain committed to protecting our clients’ interests.

Our Commitment

Since 2016, Sedikana Attorneys has remained committed to providing quality legal services at reasonable and accessible rates, while building lasting relationships with the individuals, families and communities we serve.
Our goal is not simply to represent clients in legal proceedings. It is to help people understand their rights, navigate difficult legal situations and make informed decisions about their future.
