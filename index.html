Who We Are

Why Choose Sedikana
Attorneys Inc?

We provide professional legal services with integrity, confidentiality, and a commitment to achieving the best outcomes for our clients. From the Tzaneen Magistrates Court to communities across Limpopo, we stand beside you at every step.

Professional Representation

Seasoned legal professionals who appear with authority and preparation in every court proceeding, protecting your rights with precision.

Confidential Legal Advice

What We Do

Our Practice Areas

01

Criminal Matters

Robust defence representation for all criminal charges, from bail hearings to full trials at the Magistrates Court.

02

Civil Matters

Expert handling of civil disputes, contract claims, debt recovery, and interdicts to safeguard your civil rights.

03

Unlawful Arrest & Detention

We hold authorities accountable. If your rights were violated through unlawful arrest, we fight for your justice and compensation.

RAF Claims

Navigating Road Accident Fund claims with diligence — ensuring accident victims receive fair and rightful compensation.

Litigation Services

Skilled litigation across civil and criminal matters, advocating forcefully on your behalf in court and through negotiation.

Legal Consultation

Clear, jargon-free legal advice tailored to your situation. Understand your rights and options before making any decision.
