What We Do

Our Practice Areas

Professional legal assistance across a range of criminal, civil and compensation matters in Tzaneen, Maake and surrounding areas.

01

Criminal Matters

Robust defence representation for criminal charges, including bail hearings and criminal trials at the Magistrates Court.

02

Civil Matters

Legal assistance with civil disputes, contract claims, debt recovery and interdicts to help safeguard your civil rights.

Legal assistance for matters involving unlawful arrest, detention and alleged violations of individual rights.
